# What can PadSign do? Capabilities at a glance

## Core capabilities

**Show a document on a touchscreen.** A PDF is displayed in the device's browser.
The signer can scroll and read it in full before signing anything.

**Fill in interactive forms.** If the PDF has fillable fields, the signer taps and
completes them directly on the screen. This is what removes manual data entry —
what they type is already structured data, not handwriting somebody has to
transcribe. It works with whatever fields the document contains; no per-form setup
is needed on the PadSign side.

**Capture a handwritten signature.** They draw it with a finger or a stylus, and
can clear and redo it as many times as they like before submitting.

**Place the signature into the document.** The drawn mark becomes part of the PDF
page, so anyone opening the file later sees it there.

**Apply a cryptographic seal (optional).** A separate step can seal the finished
file. TrustLynx describes this as providing "indisputable proof of authenticity
and origin". It is optional and each organisation decides whether to use it.

**Deliver the result.** The signed PDF is stored and handed onward — into a folder
your systems watch, back to the computer that sent it, and/or announced to another
system.

**Connect to your existing systems.** PadSign can work with a CRM, a medical or
healthcare information management system, a service management system, or a
document management system. Documents are generated from your own templates, and
the reference and status are reported back to whatever sent them.

## What the signer sees while this happens

The steps are shown on screen as they complete, so the person signing knows
progress is being made and when it is safe to walk away.

![A progress panel showing four signing steps with the digital stamp step active](../images/screen-progress-steps.png)

## Also available

- **Two interface languages** ship with the product: Latvian and English.
- **Automatic dark mode**, following the device's own appearance setting.
- **A signer-name label** can be shown above the signature area so the person can
  confirm the document is meant for them before signing.
- **A Windows companion app** adds a printer called "Padsign", so staff can send
  a document from any Windows application by printing it.
- **A demo mode** for evaluation, which lets you load any PDF from the device and
  sign it without a full integration.

Not everything here is switched on everywhere. Several of these are options each
organisation turns on or off when the system is set up.
