# How does signing work, start to finish?

Five stages. As the person signing, you are only involved in three of them.

## 1. The document arrives

The pad sits on a resting screen showing a logo. When a member of staff or a
connected system sends a document to that pad, it appears on its own within a few
seconds. Nobody needs to tap anything to fetch it, and you do not log in — the
device is already signed in.

## 2. You read the document

The PDF fills most of the screen. Scroll through it the way you would any page on
a tablet. Read as much as you want; nothing is submitted until you choose to
submit it.

![A document open on the signing screen with a signature box below it](../images/screen-document-view.png)

## 3. You fill in whatever it asks for

If the document has fillable fields, tap one and type into it. Highlighted areas
show where input is expected. If something required is missed, you will be told
before anything is submitted.

## 4. You sign and tap Sign

A signature box sits along the bottom of the screen with two buttons under it.
Draw your signature in the box with a finger or a stylus. **Clear** empties the box
if you want another go — you can redo it as often as you like. **Sign** submits the
document.

## 5. The system finishes the document

A progress panel replaces the buttons and shows each remaining step as it
completes: preparing the document, placing your signature into the page, applying
the cryptographic seal if your organisation uses one, and finalising. Then a
confirmation appears saying the document was signed successfully.

The pad clears itself shortly afterwards and returns to the resting screen, ready
for the next person. Your signed document has already been stored and sent onward
at that point — nothing is lost when the screen resets.

## What you do not have to do

- No app to install
- No account, username or password
- No email address to hand over just to sign
- No saving, filing or emailing the finished document — that is automatic
