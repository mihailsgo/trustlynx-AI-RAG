# What problem does PadSign solve, and what is it worth?

## The problem

Whenever a document has to be signed by someone standing in front of you, the
usual process is a loop of manual steps: generate the document, print it, hand it
over, wait, collect it, check it was filled in properly, scan it, name the file,
file it, and store the paper somewhere. Every step costs staff time. Every step
can go wrong. And until the scan happens, the information on that paper is
invisible to every other system you run.

For deliveries the problem is worse, because the paperwork travels. Physical
invoices and delivery notes get handled twice — once by your driver, once by the
customer's bookkeeping — and any last-minute change, like a missing or damaged
item, has to be written on paper and then re-entered by somebody later.

## What PadSign changes

The document is generated from your own systems and appears on a touchscreen. The
signer fills it in on the screen, so there is no transcription step and no
illegible handwriting. They sign with a finger or stylus. The signed PDF is stored
and passed on to whichever systems need it, automatically.

The information is in your systems from the moment the signature is made, not
whenever somebody gets around to scanning.

## Where the value comes from

- **Staff time.** No printing, no scanning, no filing, no chasing incomplete
  forms.
- **No paper archive.** Nothing to store, nothing to lose, nothing to retrieve by
  hand.
- **Immediate availability.** The record exists digitally at the moment of
  signing rather than hours or days later.
- **No re-keying.** What the signer typed on the screen is already data.
- **A stronger record.** With the optional seal applied, the file carries
  verifiable evidence of where it came from and shows if it has been altered
  since.
- **Fewer disputes at the point of handover.** Changes noted on the device are
  synchronised to both sides at once.

## What a customer has said

Amber Beverage Group, which produces, distributes and retails spirit beverages,
uses PadSign for goods delivery paperwork. Their Business Process Development
Manager, Mārtiņš Laizāns, described the result publicly:

> PadSign has helped us save hundreds of hours every month, eliminate paper waste,
> ensure an easy, quick, and accessible digital archive while working more
> efficiently.

He also notes that what used to be physical document handling — preparation,
processing and archiving — is now "a nearly instant data exchange in digital
format", and that signatures collected by delivery teams on a tablet synchronise
automatically into their document management and related systems.

That is one customer's published experience in one industry. It is a useful
indication of the kind of benefit PadSign is bought for, not a promise of the same
numbers elsewhere.
