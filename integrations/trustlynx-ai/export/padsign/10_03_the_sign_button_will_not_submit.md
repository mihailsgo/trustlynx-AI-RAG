# The Sign button will not submit

Three things cause this. Work through them in order.

## 1. Something required is still empty

If a required field or the signature is missing, tapping **Sign** submits nothing and a message
appears listing what is needed. That message stays on screen until you dismiss it, so it is hard
to miss — but it can appear above where you are looking if you have scrolled down the document.

**What to do:** read what the message names, scroll to those places in the document, fill them
in, then tap Sign again.

The message names what is missing but cannot always point at exactly where it is on the page, so
you may need to scroll through and look for an empty highlighted field.

## 2. The signature box is empty

The most common single cause, and the easiest to miss — particularly if you drew a signature and
then tapped **Clear** to redo it and forgot to draw the replacement.

**What to do:** look at the signature box. If there is no blue ink in it, draw your signature and
tap Sign.

A very light or very short stroke can also fail to register properly. If in doubt, tap **Clear**
and draw a fuller signature.

## 3. A submission is already running

Once you tap **Sign** successfully, the button stops responding **on purpose**. This is not a
fault — it prevents the same document being submitted twice while it is being processed.

**How to tell this is what is happening:** a progress panel will have appeared, showing the
signing steps. If you can see that panel, your document is being processed and the button is
supposed to be inert. Wait for it to finish.

**What to do:** nothing. Do not keep tapping. Wait for either a success message or a failure
message.

## Quick diagnosis

| What you see | Which cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A message listing missing items | 1 | Fill those in, tap Sign again |
| No message, empty signature box | 2 | Draw your signature |
| A progress panel with steps | 3 | Wait — it is already working |
| A progress panel that says failed | Not this problem | The signing failed; tell staff |

## If none of these fit

If the signature box has ink in it, no message is showing, and no progress panel has appeared,
tell the person at the desk. At that point it is not something the normal flow explains, and they
can check whether the pad has lost its connection.
