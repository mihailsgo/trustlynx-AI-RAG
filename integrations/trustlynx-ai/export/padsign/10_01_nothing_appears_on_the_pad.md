# Nothing appears on the pad

## First: a logo on its own is the normal resting state

The pad showing a centred logo and nothing else is not an error. It means the device is working
and waiting for a document.

![The resting screen showing only a centred logo](images/screen-idle-waiting.png)

## What to do

**1. Wait a few seconds.** The pad checks for a waiting document every few seconds. Arrival is
quick but not instantaneous.

**2. Ask whether the document was actually sent.** This is by far the most common answer.
Nothing is wrong with the pad — the document has not been sent to it yet.

**3. Ask staff to send it again.** Re-sending is always safe. There is no risk of producing two
signed copies from one re-send.

## Other explanations worth knowing

**A previous document is still on the pad.** If the screen shows somebody else's document rather
than a logo, that document needs to be dealt with or cleared first.

**The document cleared itself.** A document that arrives and is never signed clears off the pad
after about ten minutes. If you stepped away and came back to a logo, that is very likely what
happened. Just have it sent again.

**Something is wrong with the connection.** In this case you would normally see a specific
message — "Application server is not available" — rather than a logo. That message means the pad
cannot reach the system behind it. It retries automatically every ten seconds and there is a
**Retry Now** button, but this is not something a visitor can fix; tell staff.

## What not to do

**Do not keep tapping the screen.** The pad fetches documents by itself; tapping does not speed
anything up.

**Do not try to refresh or reload.** Not necessary, and it will not make a document appear that
has not been sent.

## For staff

If documents consistently fail to arrive on a particular pad, the useful distinctions are:

- Does the pad show a logo, or an "Application server is not available" message? The first
  points at the sending side, the second at the connection.
- Does it work on a different pad? That separates a device problem from a system problem.
- Was the document sent to the right account and organisation? A document sent for one
  organisation will not appear on another's pad.

## Questions this answers

- Nothing is appearing on the tablet, what do I do?
- The screen just shows a logo, is it broken?
- The document was sent but has not arrived.
- How long should I wait for the document?
- The pad seems stuck.

*Related terms: nothing appears, blank, empty screen, just a logo, not working, no document, waiting.*
