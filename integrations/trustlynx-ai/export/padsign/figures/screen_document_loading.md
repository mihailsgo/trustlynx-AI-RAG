# Screen: A spinner card reading "Loading document..." over a dimmed document

![A spinner card reading "Loading document..." over a dimmed document.](knowledge/raw/padsign/images/screen-document-loading.png)


The brief loading state, shown while the document is being fetched and rendered.

A white card sits in the middle of the screen with a circular spinner above two lines of
text: **Loading document...** and, smaller, *Please wait while we prepare your PDF*.

Behind the card the rest of the screen is faded out — the header, the document, and the
signature box with its **Clear** and **Sign** buttons are all visible but dimmed. The dimming
is what signals that nothing on the page is interactive yet. Tapping the greyed-out buttons
does nothing.

Nothing is required from the person waiting. When the document has rendered, the card
disappears and the page returns to full contrast, at which point the document can be scrolled
and the signature box used.
