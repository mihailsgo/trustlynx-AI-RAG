# Screen: A document open on the signing screen with a signature box and Clear and Sign buttons below it

![A document open on the signing screen with a signature box and Clear and Sign buttons below it.](../images/screen-document-view.png)


The main signing screen, with a document open. This is what the person signing spends most of
their time looking at.

The layout has three bands. A slim header along the top carries a small logo on the left, and
in this evaluation capture a **Load DEMO PDF** button and the signed-in account on the right.
The document fills the middle and most of the screen — here a two-page sample "Service
Agreement" from a fictional Example Corp — and scrolls up and down under a finger.

Along the bottom sits the signature area: a wide empty box with a dotted border to draw in,
and two buttons beneath it, **Clear** on the left and **Sign** on the right. That band stays
fixed in place while the document scrolls above it, so the signature controls are always
reachable without hunting for them.

Nothing is submitted until **Sign** is tapped.
