# Screen: The empty signature box with Clear and Sign buttons underneath

![The empty signature box with Clear and Sign buttons underneath.](knowledge/raw/padsign/images/screen-signature-pad.png)


The signature area, shown at the bottom of the signing screen and cropped here to show it on
its own.

A wide rectangle with a thin dotted border and a very pale fill is the signature box. This is
where you draw, with a finger or a stylus. It is empty in this capture. The box stays fixed in
place while the document scrolls above it.

Two buttons sit directly underneath, centred below the box. **Clear**, on the left, empties the
box so you can draw again — it affects only the signature, not anything typed into the
document. **Sign**, on the right, submits the document.

Nothing is sent until **Sign** is tapped, so drawing and clearing can be repeated as often as
needed. If **Sign** is tapped while the box is empty, nothing is submitted and a message
appears saying a signature is missing.
