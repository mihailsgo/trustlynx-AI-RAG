# Screen: A close-up of the document showing highlighted fillable fields, with a name and email typed in

![A close-up of the document showing highlighted fillable fields, with a name and email typed in.](knowledge/raw/padsign/images/screen-form-field-focus.png)


A close-up of part of the document, showing what fillable fields look like when the document
has them.

Under the heading "3. Your details" and the instruction "Please complete the fields below
before signing", three labelled fields are visible: **Full name**, **Email address** and
**Date**. Each is drawn as a tinted, outlined box spanning the width of the page, which is how
PadSign marks a field as something to complete — the highlight is added by the application,
not part of the printed document.

Two of the fields have been filled in with placeholder values. The third, Date, is still
empty.

Tapping a field brings up the device's own on-screen keyboard and typing goes straight into
the document. What is entered here becomes part of the signed PDF as real field data, which is
why the information does not need re-typing by staff afterwards.
