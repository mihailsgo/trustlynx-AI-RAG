# Who is TrustLynx, the company behind PadSign?

TrustLynx is a software vendor in the digital-trust space — electronic signatures, electronic
seals, identities and the services around them. PadSign is one product in its range.

## What the company says about itself

Its published mission: *"We promote adoption of trust services to make digital world more
trusted and secure."*

The idea it organises itself around is what it calls **embedded trust**: making e-identities,
e-signatures and e-seals an instinctive part of the systems people already use, rather than a
separate destination. By its own account, the company grew out of digitalisation projects —
sorting out information management and archiving inside companies' own systems, together with
e-signing, validation and time-stamping — and turned that approach into ready-to-use products.

Its published values are being customer-focused, transparent, progress-driven, and keeping a
positive working culture.

## Where they are

TrustLynx publishes contact numbers for **Latvia, Estonia and Poland** and operates its website
in English and Polish. It is a Baltic company with an EU footprint; if you need formal
corporate details (legal entity, registration, size), request them directly — they are not
published on the site and this knowledge base does not repeat unverified third-party data.

## Evidence it is real and active

Reasonable things a cautious evaluator checks, all publicly visible:

- An official website (trustlynx.com) with a maintained blog covering topics like eIDAS 2.0
- A **public developer portal** (developer.trustlynx.com) with product manuals and API
  documentation, including a PadSign user manual
- A published, attributed customer testimonial for PadSign (Amber Beverage Group)
- A named partner programme and event appearances

## The product family

PadSign sits alongside an API Platform, Connectors for business systems, SignBox (a signature
collection application), an Authentication Service and an e-Sealing service. All are built on
the same principle: deployed on the customer's own infrastructure, behind the customer's
firewalls, without sharing data with third parties.
