# What is PadSign?

PadSign is TrustLynx's e-signing solution for touchscreen devices. TrustLynx
describes it as "advanced e-Signing for touchscreen devices".

It is designed for signing **in person**. Someone is standing at your counter, or
your driver is at a delivery door, and a document needs their signature right
then. PadSign puts that document on a tablet or phone, lets them read it, fill in
whatever it asks for, and sign it with a finger or a stylus. The signed file lands
in your systems within seconds.

## What it replaces

The paper round-trip: print the form, hand it over, get it signed, scan it, file
it, and hope nobody loses the copy in between. PadSign removes every step of
that.

## What it looks like in practice

The signer sees a normal web page on the device. The document fills most of the
screen, and a signature area sits along the bottom with **Clear** and **Sign**
buttons.

![A document open on the signing screen with a signature box below it](knowledge/raw/padsign/images/screen-document-view.png)

There is nothing to install on the tablet — it runs in the device's browser.

## What it is not

- **Not a remote signing service.** It is not for emailing a document to someone
  to sign at home. The signer is physically present with your device.
- **Not a document management system.** It signs documents and hands them onward
  to wherever you keep them.
- **Not a standalone gadget.** "Pad" refers to the touchscreen device, which can
  be a tablet, a phone, or a PC with a touchscreen.

## Where it sits in the TrustLynx range

PadSign is one product in a wider set that also includes an API platform, system
connectors, SignBox, an authentication service, and a standalone e-sealing
service. PadSign is the one built specifically for face-to-face signing on a
screen.
