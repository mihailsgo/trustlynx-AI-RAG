# The document disappeared before I signed it

## What happened

A document that arrives on the pad and is not signed clears itself after about ten minutes. The
pad returns to its resting screen showing just a logo.

This is intended behaviour, not a fault and not a crash.

## Why it works that way

A pad usually sits somewhere semi-public — a counter, a waiting area. Leaving somebody's
paperwork on display indefinitely because they walked away mid-form is a bad outcome: their
details stay visible to whoever comes along next, and the next visitor finds a screen with
someone else's document on it.

So the pad clears itself. The timeout is the shortest sensible answer to "how long should a
stranger's information stay on this screen?".

## Was anything signed or saved?

**No.** Nothing was signed, and nothing was filed anywhere. A document only becomes a signed
document when you draw a signature and tap **Sign**, and then only if every step completes.

Anything you had typed into the form is gone too, which is the annoying part — but nothing
incomplete has been recorded against your name.

## What to do

Ask the person at the desk to send it again. That is all. Re-sending is safe and takes seconds.

You will need to re-enter anything you had already filled in, so it is worth completing the form
and signing in one sitting rather than leaving it part-done.

## If it keeps happening

If a document clears itself while you are actively reading it, the timeout may be set shorter
than suits the documents in use — reading a long agreement carefully can take more than ten
minutes. That is a configuration choice the organisation can change, so it is worth mentioning
to them rather than rushing through a document you are meant to read.

## Distinguishing this from other situations

| What you saw | What it means |
|---|---|
| Document was there, now just a logo | Timed out. Not signed. Send again. |
| Never saw a document at all | It was probably never sent. Ask staff. |
| Saw a success message, then it reset | **Signed successfully.** The reset after success is normal and your document is safe. |
| An "Application server is not available" message | A connection problem, not a timeout. Tell staff. |

The third row is the one worth being clear about: a reset *after* a success message is completely
different from a document vanishing before you signed. If you saw "The document has been
successfully signed", it was.
