# Who uses PadSign, and what for?

PadSign fits any organisation where somebody signs something in person. If your
staff currently hand a printed form across a counter or a clipboard out of a van,
that is the situation it was built for.

## The two main patterns

### Goods delivery

Delivery teams carry the delivery information on a phone or tablet instead of a
stack of paper invoices and delivery acts. The customer signs on the device at the
delivery point, and the details flow straight into the systems on both sides.

This pattern has a useful extra: if something changes at the last moment — items
missing, something damaged — the driver notes it on the device *before* the
signature is taken, so the correction is captured as part of the signed record
rather than scribbled on paper and re-entered later.

### Customer service desks

A visitor arrives and needs to complete paperwork. Instead of printing forms, staff
put them on a touchscreen device. The visitor fills them in and signs, and the
information is in your systems within seconds.

TrustLynx notes this is often used to capture data-processing consent, to have
someone confirm their contact and personal details are correct, and to confirm
receipt of a service.

## Documents PadSign is well suited to

- Contracts and agreements
- Onboarding paperwork for new clients or new employees
- Consent forms
- Approvals and authorisations

## Sectors

Any sector with a counter, a reception desk, or a delivery round. TrustLynx lists
these as areas its digital-trust products serve: legal services, healthcare, human
resources, financial services, logistics and supply chain, government and public
administration, technology and IT services, manufacturing, and education.

The published customer reference is Amber Beverage Group, in production,
distribution and retail of spirit beverages, using it for goods delivery. That is the only
customer TrustLynx names publicly for PadSign — no other customer can be named here, and
requests for further references or case studies should go to TrustLynx directly.

## When PadSign is the wrong fit

If the signer is not physically present — you need to send a document to someone
to sign at their own convenience, somewhere else — PadSign is not the right tool.
It is built around the signer being there with your device.
